Privacy notice

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This portfolio is operated by Sanjith Kumaar. Optional analytics help me understand how visitors use the site so I can improve its content and navigation.

Your choice

Microsoft Clarity starts only after you choose Allow analytics. Choosing Decline keeps optional analytics off and does not affect access to the portfolio, case studies, or résumé. A browser's Global Privacy Control signal also keeps optional analytics off.

Your choice is stored in your browser for up to 180 days. You can change it through Cookie settings in the footer of any page. Withdrawing permission stops further collection on this site; it does not automatically delete data already processed by Microsoft.

What analytics collect

When you allow analytics, Microsoft Clarity processes information about your visit, including:

  • Pages visited, referral information, visit duration, and device and browser details.
  • Approximate geographic information associated with the connection.
  • Clicks, taps, scrolling, and page interactions used to create heatmaps and session replays.
  • Interactions such as opening the résumé, exploring case studies, or clicking contact links.

Session replays reconstruct activity within this website. They do not record your webcam, microphone, or other desktop applications. Sensitive text, including form inputs and email addresses, is masked by Clarity's default settings. I do not send visitors' names or email addresses as custom identifiers.

Cookies and Microsoft

Clarity uses cookies such as _clck and _clsk to connect interactions into visits. Analytics permission is sent to Clarity only after you allow it; advertising storage permission remains denied.

Microsoft processes Clarity information according to its own terms and privacy practices. Read the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Clarity data-retention information for details.

Other websites and contact

LinkedIn, Figma, and other external links or embedded services follow their own privacy practices. This notice covers analytics on sanjithuiux.design.

Privacy notice

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This portfolio is operated by Sanjith Kumaar. Cookieless analytics help me understand how visitors use the site so I can improve its content and navigation.

How analytics work

Microsoft Clarity loads automatically in cookieless mode to measure page views and basic interactions. No analytics popup or confirmation click is required. Analytics and advertising storage permissions are sent to Clarity as denied.

Clarity does not use its analytics cookies to connect visits in this mode. Activity on different pages may count as separate sessions, and continuous session replays and returning-visitor identification are limited.

A browser's Global Privacy Control signal keeps Clarity off. A still-valid analytics opt-out saved under the previous version of this site is also respected.

What analytics collect

In cookieless mode, Microsoft Clarity processes basic information about your visit, including:

  • Pages visited, referral information, page-level activity, and device and browser details.
  • Approximate geographic information associated with the connection.
  • Clicks, taps, scrolling, and page interactions used for behavioral analytics. Features that require linked visits or full session replays are limited.
  • Interactions such as opening the résumé, exploring case studies, or clicking contact links.

Session replays reconstruct activity within this website. They do not record your webcam, microphone, or other desktop applications. Sensitive text, including form inputs and email addresses, is masked by Clarity's default settings. I do not send visitors' names or email addresses as custom identifiers.

Cookies and Microsoft

The site does not grant Clarity permission to use analytics or advertising cookies. First-party Clarity cookies from the previous opt-in setup are cleared. Cookieless operation still involves processing usage and connection information; it does not mean no data is collected.

Microsoft processes Clarity information according to its own terms and privacy practices. Read the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Clarity data-retention information for details.

DREXEL WORKS - CAMPUS JOB SEARCH APP | Drexel university Project

Sep 2025 - Mar 2026

Independently designed a campus job-search app concept for Drexel students, covering user research, wireframing, visual design, prototyping, and usability testing.

  • User research: Surveyed 30 students and interviewed 15 to identify challenges with scattered listings, unclear eligibility, and outdated job information.
  • Research synthesis: Organized findings into student needs and design priorities. Focused the concept on bringing listings together, clarifying who could apply, presenting consistent job details, and helping students follow their applications.
  • User flows and wireframes: Developed flows for discovering jobs, reviewing requirements, applying, and tracking progress. Created wireframes to establish screen layouts, navigation, and the placement of information needed at each step.
  • Design and prototyping: Built an interactive Figma prototype with eligibility badges, job filters, and an application dashboard.
  • Usability testing: Tested with 15 students to evaluate job discovery, eligibility clarity, and completion of application tasks.
